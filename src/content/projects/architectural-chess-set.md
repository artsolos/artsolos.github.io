## The problem

This SolidWorks Skillbuilder assignment had my teammate and me model a full chess set, with each piece inspired by a famous building. Architecture isn't my main interest. The value was in the modeling itself: turning curved and repeated forms into clean features.

## My role

I modeled three pieces. My teammate modeled the rest, and the full board is shared work.

## My pieces

- **Pawn, the Roman Colosseum.** The pawn is the most numerous piece, so it got a simple, repeatable form: a ring of arches on a faceted base.
- **Bishop, the Washington Monument.** A tall, tapering obelisk, a nod to the bishop's long diagonal moves.
- **Rook, the Sydney Opera House.** Overlapping shell "sails" on a sturdy base for the defensive piece.

## Design idea

Each building was matched to how its piece behaves on the board, not just how it looks. The many pawns got the simplest repeated form. The bishop got the tallest, narrowest shape for a piece that moves in long diagonals. The rook got the most solid, enclosed shape for a defensive piece.

## Result

A complete themed set, with my three pieces modeled in SolidWorks and assembled on the shared board.
